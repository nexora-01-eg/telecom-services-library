# ROLLZ: THE HERO WHO CLOCKED OUT

## Five-Video Editing Plan

The 40 vertical images are divided into five short-form videos, with eight frames per video. Each episode should be delivered in **9:16 vertical format**, with a target duration of **40–55 seconds**. Use gentle parallax, slow push-ins, controlled zooms, and short lateral camera moves rather than aggressive animation. Add all English dialogue and captions during editing for clean readability.

| Video | Frames | Story function | Suggested duration | Ending hook |
|---|---:|---|---:|---|
| Video 1 — The Last Shift | 01–08 | Introduce Captain Crave, his exhaustion, and Rollz as his sanctuary. | 45–50 sec | The Deadline signal appears outside Rollz. |
| Video 2 — The City That Would Not Sleep | 09–16 | Reveal the threat and lead the team beneath Rollz. | 45–55 sec | A secret staircase opens under the counter. |
| Video 3 — The Engine Under Rollz | 17–24 | Reveal the origin of The Deadline and Captain Crave’s emotional crisis. | 50–55 sec | The countdown begins as the team enters the machine. |
| Video 4 — The Ten-Minute War | 25–32 | Show the climax, shared responsibility, and defeat of The Deadline. | 50–60 sec | The city’s stolen time returns. |
| Video 5 — Clocked Out | 33–40 | Resolve the story and turn Rollz into a community sanctuary. | 45–55 sec | Final Rollz hero image and campaign line. |

## Video 1 — The Last Shift

Use frames 01–08 in order. Begin with a fast three-beat montage of Metrovale, the runaway bus, and the impossible emergency list. Slow the pace on frame 04 so the audience feels Captain Crave’s decision. Transition into Rollz with warm amber light and a soft restaurant ambience. Frame 07 should receive the longest hold, allowing the sandwich to become the emotional object of the story.

Suggested English voice-over:

> “For ten years, Captain Crave saved Metrovale every night. But tonight, he wanted something impossible. One quiet table. One favorite sandwich. One night off.”

Frame 40 should be a clean final hold of at least four seconds.

Suggested English voice-over:

> “Metrovale did not need one hero to carry everything. It needed a place where everyone could stop, breathe, and remember they were human.”

Use warm guitar, soft brushed drums, light room ambience, and a gentle closing melody. End with the sound of a restaurant door bell and a final low musical note under the Rollz exterior.

## Caption and Branding Rules

Keep all spoken English captions short and high-contrast. Place captions in a consistent lower safe zone, leaving faces and the sandwich unobstructed. Use a clean bold sans-serif typeface in white with a black outline or on a semi-transparent black panel. Add the accurate **ROLLZ** logo or wordmark during editing rather than relying on generated lettering. Keep the yellow-and-black restaurant palette visible in every Rollz shot.
